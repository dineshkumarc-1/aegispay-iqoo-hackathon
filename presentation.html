01. The Problem Statement Real-World Threat Landscape

The ₹1,750+ Crore Digital Payment Epidemic

India processes over 14+ Billion UPI transactions monthly, creating an urgent hunting ground for cyber criminals.

1. Physical QR Quishing Stickers

Fraudsters paste malicious stickers over genuine merchant QR codes at chai stalls and retail counters, routing payments directly to disposable mule accounts.

2. Reverse PIN & Social Engineering

Victims are psychologically coerced during phone calls (e.g. fake electricity cutoff, OLX buyers) to enter their 6-digit PIN under the claim of receiving refunds or cashback.

3. Counterfeit Receipt Spoof Apps

Spoofed payment confirmation apps display fake green checkmarks and simulated audio soundbox chimes, duping small Kirana store merchants daily.

The Victim Profile: 68% of victims are first-time digital citizens, elderly users, and vernacular speakers in Tier-2/3 cities who lack cybersecurity awareness.
02. The Existing Gap Why Cloud Defenses Fail

Why Cloud-Based Fraud Prevention is Inadequate

Latency Bottleneck (800ms – 2,500ms)

Fraud happens in split-seconds. Roundtrips to cloud APIs cannot intercept user taps before money leaves the bank. Preventative defense must execute locally in <15ms.

Data Privacy & DPDP Act Violations

Transmitting user SMS messages, OTPs, and personal VPA transaction histories to remote servers introduces serious data leakage and non-compliance risks.

Offline & Rural Market Blindspot

Cloud fraud verification fails completely in basements, underground metro stations, rural fairs, and poor 2G/3G connectivity zones.

Passive Warning Banners Are Ignored

Static text warning banners are systematically overlooked by victims under high emotional panic or urgency manipulation.
