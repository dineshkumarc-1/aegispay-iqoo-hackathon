01. The Problem Statement FinTech & Commerce Risk Landscape

The ₹1,750+ Crore UPI & Retail Payment Crisis

India processes 14+ Billion monthly UPI transactions, but 98% of digital fraud happens at the consumer-merchant interface.

1. Retail QR Quishing & Tamper

Malicious stickers pasted over authentic merchant QR stands divert everyday retail commerce payments directly to disposable mule bank accounts.

2. Reverse Collect & AutoPay Traps

Victims are tricked via fake calls to enter their 6-digit PIN to "receive" refund credits, or approve deceptive ₹9,999/month recurring e-Mandates.

3. Kirana Merchant Receipt Fraud

Spoofed payment confirmation apps display fake green checkmarks and simulated audio soundbox chimes to steal goods from small retail shopkeepers.

The Market Vulnerability: 68% of victims are first-time digital citizens, elderly users, and vernacular speakers in Tier-2/3 retail markets.
02. The Existing Gap Why Cloud Bank APIs Fail

Why Cloud-Based Fraud Prevention Fails in FinTech

Latency Bottleneck (1,200ms – 3,000ms)

UPI transactions happen in split-seconds. Cloud API roundtrips cannot stop an impulsive victim tap before funds leave the bank. Preventative defense must execute locally in <15ms.

Data Privacy (DPDP Act Compliance)

Transmitting user SMS messages, OTPs, and personal VPA transaction histories to remote servers introduces serious financial data breach liabilities.

Offline & Rural Commerce Blindspots

Cloud verification fails in basements, underground metro stations, rural fairs, and poor 2G/3G connectivity zones where millions of daily cash-to-UPI trades occur.

Passive Text Warning Banners Are Ignored

Static text warning banners are systematically overlooked by victims under high emotional panic or urgency manipulation.
