01. The Problem

The ₹1,750+ Crore Digital Payment Epidemic

Over 14 Billion UPI transactions happen monthly in India, but digital fraud has exploded proportionally.

1. QR Quishing Stickers

Fraudsters paste fake QR stickers over tea stall merchant codes, routing funds into mule accounts.

2. Reverse PIN Deception

Scammers trick users into entering their UPI PIN under the claim of "receiving" lottery or refund credits.

3. Counterfeit Receipt Apps

Spoofed payment confirmation apps display fake green ticks & soundbox tones to dupe retail shopkeepers.

02. Market Gap

Why Existing Cloud Security Fails

Cloud Latency (1-3 Seconds)

Fraud happens in split-seconds. Cloud roundtrips cannot stop an impulsive user tap before money leaves the bank.

Privacy Violations (DPDP Act)

Sending sensitive SMS, OTPs, and payment handles to remote servers introduces serious data leakage risks.

Offline Blindspots

Cloud verification fails completely in basements, rural markets, and poor network zones.

Passive Warning Banners

Static text banners are routinely ignored by victims under active psychological manipulation.

03. The Solution

Three-Pillar Preventative Defense Engine

1

Dynamic QR & Quishing Shield

Real-time optical AR parser verifying UPI deep-links, VPA entropy, and blocking disguised collect requests.

2

Social Engineering Interceptor

Quantized on-device NLP model analyzing calls/SMS in real time to catch panic baits & AnyDesk remote-access scams.

3

Receipt & Soundbox Guard

Instant CV font-kerning, UTR checksum, and audio-tone verification for Kirana store cashiers.
